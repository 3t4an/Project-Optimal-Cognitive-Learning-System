## What Went Well

- Non-Linearity
	- Like before, I was fairly easy to pick taking notes in non-linearity.
	- Creating a note that is non-linear allows for more relationship and multi-dimensional ideas.

## What Went Poorly

- Creation Hierarchy
	- I focused on creating the perfect mind map first without getting my thoughts out fully.
	- This caused me to be lost when placing down new concepts because they could apply do different sections
	- **So...** Next time I will focus on finding big ideas in sections WHILE reading not before.
	- While conducting preliminary I will focus on creating hypothesis and predictions, not building out the mind map.
- Metacognition
	- During the reading and building, I was focusing too hard on thinking about thinking.
	- "How does this connect?", "Does this make sense?", "Is it perfect?"
	- This caused me to freeze overload my cognitive thinking.
	- **So...** I should just focus strictly on potential questions and **grouping**.
	- I should find the main idea of a section and build details off that.
	- Allow for cross-grouping relationships.
- Depth of Detail
	- I thought too hard about the details
	- They should just reveal themselves as I branch off from a main idea.
- Space
	- I often ran out of space on the page.
	- I should allows myself to go into other pages.
- Retainment
	- When answering questions I couldn't recall any of the information I had read or written on my map.
	- I realized that I was because I never been asking myself questions about the topic.
	- I had only been re-writing what the textbook said in a webbed format.
	- **So...** I need to ask myself questions and create logical chain for more relationships.
