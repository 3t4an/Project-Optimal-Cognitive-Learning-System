## What went well

- Non-Linearity
	- I picked up on the ability to write non-linearly fair quickly.
	- Within the first 10 minutes, branching off into different section and ideas wasn't so hard.
- First Stages of Metacognition
	- I was constantly asking myself "why" when building my mind-map.
	- This helped me go further deeper into my topics and explaining why this why that.


## What did not go so well

- Time Management
	- Because this is a new skill, it took me lots of time to get my thoughts out on paper.
	- This ultimately led to 2 hours being spent on developing this new skill.
	- **So...** this is a process of develpoing a new skill and more practice will increase fluidity.
- Metacognition
	- When reading the content, it was difficult for me to think about grouping, relationships, direction, formatting all at once.
	- **So...** I should approach each of these complex skills, according to [Justin Sung's G.R.I.N.D.E checklist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Grd7K7bJVWg), one by one and getting comfortable with thinking while/about thinking.
- Space
	- Because I was using paper, I often ran out of space and had to cram things into the corner of the page or completely start a new topic on another page.
	- **So...** I should work on my organization to try and use less words and better formatting. Especially considering I have to use paper in high school.
