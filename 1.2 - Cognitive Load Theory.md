### Explains our mental capacity and the amount of stress put on our "thinking" parts.

> States that, within the Short-Term Memory, we can only hold on to about 7 ± 2 items for about 10-20 seconds without rehearsing.

- Intrinsic Load
	- Comes from the complexity of the material itself and the amount of things required to process simultaneously in order to understand.
- Extraneous Load
	- Comes from the poor direction or instruction from a certain material.
	- Forces the brain to make sense of the material before diving in.
- Germane Load (Good Load)
	- Comes from the brain's expenditure of energy from thinking, recalling, learning, etc.
