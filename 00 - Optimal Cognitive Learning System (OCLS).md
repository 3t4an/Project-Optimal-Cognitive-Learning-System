# Optimal Cognitive Learning System (OCLS)

> The OCLS project is going to develop a learning system that flows with the brain natural way of learning. It will stimulate these processes to promote deep understanding, memory retainment and retrieval. 

**Cognitive Learning Theory** - "Cognitive learning theory is an approach to learning that centers on internal mental processes. It explains how learners take in information, organize it, store it in memory, and retrieve it when needed." [Western Governors University](https://www.wgu.edu/blog/what-is-cognitive-learning2003.html)

**Learn** - "to gain knowledge or understanding of or skill in by study, instruction, or experience" [Merriam Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/learn)

**System** - "a regularly interacting or interdependent group of items forming a unified whole" [Merriam Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/system)

## Step 1 - Learning

- Understand Cognitive Learning Theory and the learning processes of the brain [[1.0 - Information Processing Theory.canvas]]
- Understand the different steps the brain takes into digesting information.

## Step 2 - Comparing

- Take your understanding of Cognitive Learning Theory and compare it to other learning methods and systems
- Understand how each of them flow within CLT to maximize learning
- Understand how each of them "activate" each step into promoting memory retainment and deep understanding

## Step 3 - Developing

- Create the first draft of a system that flows and interacts with the human learning processes in order to promote deeper understanding.
- Create a system that stimulates each step in the learning process.
- Take inspiration and try's from other learning systems.
- Understand why certain "study techniques" fail and learning system prevail.

## Step 4 - Testing

- After developing the first draft, test it.
- Find the system weaknesses and strengths
- Record the findings of testing and compare.
- Complete iterations until satisfactory results.

## Step 5 - Finalization

- Tweak and adjust the method in accordance to the strengths and weaknesses found during testing.
- The final product should be a personalized system that, when applied during learn, can adapt to different situations and promote a deeper understanding of the material during each step.
- The created system should optimize learning using, and flowing with, cognitive science, not against it.
